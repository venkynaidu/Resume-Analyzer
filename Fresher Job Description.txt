Job Title: Junior AI & Data Science Engineer – Fresher

Job Description:

We are looking for enthusiastic and motivated fresh graduates to join our team as Junior AI & Data Science Engineers. Candidates should have a strong foundation in Python, data science, machine learning, and artificial intelligence and a willingness to learn new technologies.

Responsibilities:
- Assist in developing machine learning and data science projects.
- Collect, clean, preprocess, and analyze datasets.
- Develop basic machine learning models using Python and Scikit-learn.
- Perform exploratory data analysis and create meaningful visualizations.
- Assist senior team members in developing AI and Generative AI applications.
- Work with Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, and Scikit-learn.
- Write SQL queries to retrieve and analyze data.
- Participate in model evaluation and performance improvement.
- Develop small AI/ML projects as part of the development team.
- Learn and work with modern AI technologies including LLMs, RAG, LangChain, and AI agents.
- Maintain project documentation and code using Git/GitHub.
- Collaborate with team members and participate in technical discussions.

Required Skills:
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, Engineering, Mathematics, Statistics, or a related field.
- Basic knowledge of Python programming.
- Understanding of Machine Learning concepts.
- Knowledge of Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, and Scikit-learn.
- Basic knowledge of SQL and databases.
- Understanding of data preprocessing and exploratory data analysis.
- Basic understanding of statistics and probability.
- Familiarity with Git and GitHub.
- Good analytical and problem-solving skills.
- Strong interest in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science.

Preferred Skills:
- Academic or personal projects in AI, Machine Learning, or Data Science.
- Basic knowledge of Deep Learning and NLP.
- Familiarity with Generative AI and Large Language Models.
- Exposure to LangChain, RAG, or AI agents.
- Familiarity with Google Gemini or other LLM APIs.
- Internship or certification in AI, Machine Learning, or Data Science.

Job Title: AI & Data Science Engineer

Job Description:

We are looking for an experienced AI & Data Science Engineer to join our team. The ideal candidate should have hands-on experience in Python, machine learning, deep learning, data analysis, and generative AI.

Responsibilities:
- Develop and deploy machine learning and deep learning models for real-world business problems.
- Analyze large datasets and identify meaningful patterns and insights.
- Build predictive models using supervised and unsupervised learning techniques.
- Develop AI and Generative AI applications using Large Language Models (LLMs).
- Work with frameworks such as LangChain, TensorFlow, PyTorch, Scikit-learn, and Pandas.
- Develop and integrate REST APIs for AI/ML applications.
- Work with vector databases and implement Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) applications.
- Perform data preprocessing, feature engineering, model evaluation, and hyperparameter tuning.
- Deploy machine learning and AI applications using cloud platforms such as AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud.
- Collaborate with software engineers, data engineers, and business teams.
- Monitor and improve the performance of deployed AI/ML models.

Required Skills:
- 3+ years of experience in AI, Machine Learning, or Data Science.
- Strong programming skills in Python.
- Strong knowledge of Machine Learning and Deep Learning.
- Experience with NLP, Generative AI, and LLM applications.
- Hands-on experience with LangChain and RAG.
- Experience with TensorFlow or PyTorch.
- Strong knowledge of Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn, and SQL.
- Experience with REST APIs and Git/GitHub.
- Experience working with cloud platforms such as AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.

Preferred Skills:
- Experience with OpenAI or Google Gemini APIs.
- Experience with vector databases such as FAISS, Chroma, Pinecone, or Weaviate.
- Experience developing AI agents and agentic AI applications.
- Experience with Docker and CI/CD.
- Experience deploying production-grade AI applications.
